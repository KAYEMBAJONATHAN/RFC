About RFC INTERNATION

RFC INTERNATION was founded on 27th October 2013 by Senior Pastor Reverend Doxa Kalenga Disashi, a visionary leader and passionate servant of Christ.

Reverend Doxa is also the founder of Young Winners in Jesus-Christ Ministries, a movement dedicated to empowering youth through faith, purpose, and community.

RFC INTERNATION continues to grow as a vibrant church family, committed to transforming lives through the Word, worship, and outreach.
